About 2Sloo

2Sloo creates free settings, presets, graphics configurations, force-feedback setups, and vehicle setups for sim racing and driving games — Assetto Corsa, Assetto Corsa EVO, Assetto Corsa Rally, and BeamNG.drive, with more on the way.

This site is where that work gets shared — free, no accounts, no catch.

ModsFire is the recommended download host because it helps fund future releases, but every setting on this site is and always will be free to download.
