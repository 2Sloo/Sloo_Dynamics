> **Free projects, settings, and tools for the sim-racing community.**

Sloo Dynamics is my personal hub for the projects, mods, graphics, settings, and other stuff I make for racing games and the sim-racing community.

I mainly make things for games like **BeamNG.drive** and the **Assetto Corsa franchise**, ranging from graphics packs and liveries to force feedback settings, vehicle setups, and other quality-of-life projects — **and everything I make is completely free for everyone to use.**

The goal of Sloo Dynamics is pretty simple:

**Make cool stuff. Make it easy to use. And give it to the community without putting it behind a paywall.**

## 🛠️ About the Project

Sloo Dynamics is a personal project created and maintained by **2Sloo**.

A lot of the projects here come from things I've wanted to improve or create while playing and working with racing games myself. Some projects are small experiments, while others are developed into full releases.

Everything is built with the goal of being **useful, accessible, and actually worth using.**

Most importantly, **I don't believe people should have to pay just to enjoy a better sim-racing experience.** That's why the projects I release through Sloo Dynamics are made available for free.
